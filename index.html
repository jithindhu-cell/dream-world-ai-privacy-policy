Privacy Policy for Dream World AI

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Dream World AI ("the app", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Dream World AI accesses, uses, processes, and protects information when you use the app.

1. Information We Access

Dream World AI may access photos that you choose to provide through your device's photo gallery or other supported image-selection features.

We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, address, or other profile information in order to use the app.

2. Photos and Images

When you use AI photo features such as Face Swap or other image-generation features, images that you select may be transmitted securely to our processing service so that the requested AI operation can be performed.

Images are used for the purpose of providing the requested AI photo functionality. We do not use your personal photos for unrelated purposes.

3. AI Processing Service

Dream World AI uses an online processing service to perform certain AI image-processing operations. Information required to perform the requested operation may be transmitted to this service.

The processing service is used only to provide the requested functionality, such as generating or processing images.

4. Advertising

Dream World AI may display advertisements using Google AdMob. Advertising services may collect certain device or advertising-related information in accordance with Google's privacy practices.

Advertising information may include device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location information, app interaction information, and other information required for advertising, fraud prevention, measurement, or related services, depending on Google's services and applicable settings.

5. Information We Do Not Collect

Dream World AI does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect passwords, contacts, messages, or call history.

6. How We Use Information

Information accessed or transmitted by the app may be used to:

  • Provide AI photo-processing features.
  • Process photos selected by the user.
  • Display and operate advertisements.
  • Maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the service.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with service providers when necessary to provide app functionality, including AI image processing services and advertising services such as Google AdMob.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information transmitted through the app. Data transmitted between the app and our online services is intended to be protected using secure network communication such as HTTPS/TLS.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, maintain security, troubleshoot technical problems, comply with legal obligations, or fulfill legitimate operational needs.

Because Dream World AI does not require user accounts, there is no user account profile stored by the app that users need to close or deactivate.

If you have a privacy or data-deletion request concerning information associated with your use of the service, you may contact us using the developer contact information provided on the Dream World AI Google Play listing.

10. Children's Privacy

Dream World AI is not specifically directed to children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for account creation.

11. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services, including Google AdMob and online AI-processing infrastructure. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to make a privacy or data-deletion request, please contact us at jithindhu@gmail.com.
