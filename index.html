Privacy Policy for Dream World AI Studio

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Dream World AI Studio ("the app", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app accesses, uses, processes, and protects information when you use its features.

1. Information We Access

The app may access images that you select or provide within its supported AI photo features, including images needed for Face Swap. The app does not offer the previous feature that allowed users to upload any arbitrary photo from the phone gallery as a target photo.

The app does not require you to create an account or provide your name, address, or other profile information in order to use its features.

2. Photos and Images

When you use features such as Single Face Swap, Couple/Double Face Swap, or AI image generation, images you select or provide for the requested operation may be transmitted securely to our online AI-processing service. The service processes those images to provide the feature you requested.

Images are used to provide the requested AI photo functionality and to maintain and secure the service. Please do not submit images unless you have the right and permission to use them.

3. AI Processing Service

Dream World AI Studio uses an online processing service to perform certain AI image-processing operations. Images and other information required to perform the requested operation may be transmitted to this service.

The processing service is used to provide requested functionality, such as face swapping and AI image generation. The processing provider may process information under its own terms and privacy practices.

4. Advertising

The app may display advertisements using Google AdMob. Advertising services may collect certain device or advertising-related information in accordance with Google's privacy practices.

Advertising information may include device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location information, app interaction information, and other information required for advertising, fraud prevention, measurement, or related services, depending on Google's services and applicable settings.

5. Information We Do Not Collect

The app does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect passwords, contacts, messages, or call history.

6. How We Use Information

Information accessed or transmitted by the app may be used to:

  • Provide AI photo-processing features, including Face Swap and AI image generation.
  • Process images selected or provided for the requested feature.
  • Display and operate advertisements, where enabled.
  • Maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the service.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with service providers when necessary to provide app functionality, including online AI image processing services and advertising services such as Google AdMob.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information transmitted through the app. Data transmitted between the app and our online services is intended to be protected using secure network communication such as HTTPS/TLS.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, maintain security, troubleshoot technical problems, comply with legal obligations, or fulfill legitimate operational needs. Retention of images processed by the online AI service may also depend on that provider's retention practices.

Because the app does not require user accounts, there is no user account profile stored by the app that users need to close or deactivate.

For privacy or data-deletion requests concerning information associated with your use of the service, contact us using the developer contact information provided on the app's Google Play listing.

10. Children's Privacy

Dream World AI Studio is not specifically directed to children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for account creation.

11. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services, including Google AdMob and online AI-processing infrastructure. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to make a privacy or data-deletion request, please contact us at jithindhu@gmail.com.
